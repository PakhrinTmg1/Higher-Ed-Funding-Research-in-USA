# U.S. Higher Education Funding Research

This paper examines how U.S. colleges and universities are financed and what they spend money on, using fiscal year 2024 data from the National Center for Education Statistics (NCES) Integrated Postsecondary Education Data System (IPEDS).

## What it covers
- Revenue and expense breakdowns for public four-year, public two-year, private nonprofit, and private for-profit institutions
- How reliance on government funding, tuition, investment income, and gifts differs by institution type (for example, government support is about 33.6% of revenue at public four-year institutions and about 73.1% at public two-year institutions)
- A policy proposal: a $50 billion annual increase in public investment, targeted at need-based grants, community colleges, public four-year funding, student success programs, and emergency aid

## Data source
NCES, IPEDS Table 5: Amount and percentage distribution of revenues and expenses at Title IV institutions.
